HOW CIRCUIT LEARNING WORKS

The first completed lap creates provisional ⅓ and ⅔ checkpoint anchors. Repeated laps confirm them. If two later laps consistently follow another path, Pitlane can replace a bad provisional learning lap while keeping the earlier lap in history.

SAFETY & PRIVACY
AFILÉON PITLANE // TERMS & SAFETY v1.0

Use the app. Not while driving.

Pitlane is a track-day companion and approximate timing tool. It is not official competition timing, a safety system, driver coaching, or a substitute for circuit instructions or marshals.

Driver safety

Configure the app, mount the device securely and make any selections before the vehicle moves. The driver must not tap, type, read, adjust or otherwise interact with Pitlane while driving. Manual lap timing is intended only for a passenger, pit crew member, or stationary use. Never use Pitlane in a way that distracts from driving.

Closed-circuit use

Automatic lap timing is intended for lawful closed-circuit use where timing devices are permitted. Do not use Pitlane to encourage speeding or competitive driving on public roads. Follow the circuit operator's rules at all times.

Timing accuracy

Phone GPS and external receivers can drift, lose signal or produce inaccurate results. Times, checkpoints, speed and circuit recognition are estimates and must not be relied on for official results, safety-critical decisions or vehicle control.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for safe driving, vehicle condition, device mounting, circuit compliance and deciding whether it is safe and lawful to use the app. Stop using Pitlane if it interferes with safe operation of the vehicle.

Location & privacy

During an active automatic timing session Pitlane processes precise location on the device. Full route history is not saved. For learned layouts Pitlane may save only the start/finish anchor and two derived checkpoint anchors, together with lap/session results. Location is not used for advertising.

Liability

Pitlane is provided as a convenience tool and may contain errors or become unavailable. To the extent permitted by law, Afiléon Motorsport is not responsible for losses caused by reliance on approximate timing or by use contrary to these safety instructions. Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts liability where the law does not permit that exclusion or restriction.

PRECISE LOCATION DISCLOSURE

GPS timing needs location.

During an active GPS timing session, Afiléon Pitlane uses your precise location to detect start/finish and checkpoint crossings and calculate lap times. The full route is processed temporarily in memory and discarded; Pitlane saves lap results and a small number of circuit anchor points, not your continuous location history.

Pitlane does not use location for advertising. This build requests location only while you are using the app.

MANUAL TIMING

Passenger / crew only while moving.

The large manual lap button is designed for a passenger or pit crew member. A driver must not use it while the vehicle is moving.
